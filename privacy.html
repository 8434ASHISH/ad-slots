🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 23, 2026

Important: UtraShare P2P uses direct peer-to-peer technology. We do NOT store any personal data, files, or browsing history on our servers.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

UtraShare is built on WebRTC peer-to-peer technology. This means:

• We do NOT store your files on any server
• We do NOT collect personal identification information
• We do NOT track your browsing history
• We do NOT store transfer logs or metadata

2. How P2P Transfer Works

All file transfers happen directly between your browser and the recipient's browser. Our servers only facilitate the initial connection (signaling) and are not involved in the actual data transfer.

3. Third-Party Advertisements

We display advertisements through third-party networks (Monetag and Adsterra). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads. Please review their respective privacy policies:

• Monetag Privacy Policy
• Adsterra Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

Since no data is stored on our servers, there is no centralized database to breach. Your files remain private and secure during transfer.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@utrashare.com
